    Pauli transfer matrices

    All matrices are in the gate's own Schur frame, which for √X is the usual I, X, Y, Z basis. PTM shows each estimate next to the ideal and true matrices; PTM residual shows each estimate minus the true matrix, on its own colour scale. RB has no PTM. QSQ returns only the gauge-invariant eigenvalues, and cannot see the sign of the over-rotation entry a (the Y–Y and Z–Z entries), so its panels show |a| and are compared with the truth folded the same way.

    What to look for

    • QPT assumes perfect state preparation and readout, so readout error ends up in the gate and it usually reports a far worse gate than the true one. Long-sequence QPT repeats the gate up to 257 times, which spreads that error thinly.
    • GST fits preparation, readout and gates together. Short GST (no repeated germs) has no amplification, so a 0.1% error can sit below its shot noise.
    • RB ignores readout error, but its per-gate number averages √X with √Y. A coherent over-rotation of √X makes interleaved RB overestimate the error.
    • QSQ regressors map the failure rates of a few repeated-gate sequences to the gate's eigenvalues. The sets differ in which sequences they use: greedy 8 and 15, greedy with shorter sequences preferred, a structured ladder of lengths, and a 26-sequence baseline.
